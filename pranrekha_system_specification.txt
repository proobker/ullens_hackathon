## 1. PROJECT CONTEXT & CORE PHILOSOPHY

### The Problem: The "Golden Hour" Bottleneck
During severe medical emergencies, the first 60 minutes are critical. Currently, 30–60 minutes are lost during transit and ER intake because emergency physicians cannot trust self-reported medical ID apps (e.g., Apple Health, basic QR stickers). To avoid fatal malpractice, doctors run repetitive bedside blood-typing and allergy cross-matches, causing fatal delays.

### The PranRekha Solution
PranRekha is a verified digital emergency registry that achieves **Zero-Delay Emergency Triage**.
1. **Clinical Authentication Only:** Patients cannot self-edit their medical records. Only government-accredited diagnostic labs and clinics can cryptographically sign and update records.
2. **Break-Glass Pre-Arrival Triage:** Paramedics scan a patient's physical/digital PranRekha QR code. This triggers a "Break-Glass" emergency override, instantly streaming the patient's verified baseline data to the destination hospital's ER dashboard *before the ambulance arrives*.
3. **Preventative Habit Loop (Business Model):** Dynamic data (blood pressure, vitals) expires every 6 months. The app prompts patients to book routine checkups at partner clinics, generating a commission/take-rate for the platform.

## 4. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS: THE 4 PORTALS

You must build a master navigation controller (a persistent bottom or top bar) allowing the user to seamlessly toggle between the 4 core views for a live hackathon demo.

### View 1: Patient Dashboard (`/patient`)
* **Objective:** Display the digital emergency pass and drive checkup retention.
* **UI Components:**
  * **Digital Emergency Card:** High-contrast QR code, large `PR-XXXX-XXXX` ID, and immediate display of Blood Group and Donor Status.
  * **Clinical Verification Badges:** Every medical data point must have a tiny shield icon or text saying "Verified by [Facility Name]".
  * **Freshness Radar:** A prominent visual indicator (Green/Yellow/Red) showing the countdown until the dynamic data expires.
  * **Call to Action:** A "Book 6-Month Recertification Checkup" button that shows a partner discount (e.g., "$45 Checkup - 15% PranRekha Discount").

### View 2: Paramedic Field Scanner (`/paramedic`)
* **Objective:** Trigger the Break-Glass protocol and alert the ER.
* **UI Components:**
  * **Scanner Mockup:** A camera viewfinder UI with a button to "Simulate QR Scan".
  * **Break-Glass Authentication:** Once scanned, show a restricted view requiring the paramedic to enter their Unit ID and select a trauma category (e.g., "Unconscious Polytrauma").
  * **Pre-Arrival Dispatch Button:** A massive, high-contrast button labeled **"Initiate Pre-Arrival ER Stream"**. Clicking this uses `BroadcastChannel` to send a `TriageAlert` payload to the ER Dashboard. Includes a slider to set ETA (e.g., "Arriving in 12 mins").

### View 3: ER Trauma Dashboard (`/hospital`)
* **Objective:** Receive live alerts and prepare the trauma bay before arrival.
* **UI Components:**
  * **Live Alert Feed:** A dark-mode, high-contrast Kanban-style board or list of incoming ambulances.
  * **Real-Time Listener:** Listens via `BroadcastChannel`. When an alert is received, trigger a visual flash/pulse and audio cue (if possible).
  * **Actionable Medical Directives:** Automatically translate the incoming data into ER instructions. (e.g., If Blood Group is `O-`, show massive text: "PREPARE 2 UNITS O- NEGATIVE BLOOD").
  * **Contraindication Warnings:** Flashing red banners for fatal allergies (e.g., "DO NOT ADMINISTER PENICILLIN").

### View 4: Diagnostic Lab Portal (`/lab`)
* **Objective:** The data-entry point that prevents self-reporting and generates revenue.
* **UI Components:**
  * **Doctor Login Mockup:** A simple state showing the clinician is logged in.
  * **Data Entry Form:** Inputs to update a patient's blood pressure, vitals, and medications.
  * **Cryptographic Sign-off:** A button to "Sign & Commit Records to PranRekha Network".
  * **Revenue Ledger:** A small dashboard showing recent checkups processed and the platform commission generated (e.g., "Checkup: $50 | PranRekha Take-Rate (8%): $4.00").
